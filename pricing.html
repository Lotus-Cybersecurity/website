Assessment Workflow

How a security assessment works

The goal is not to dump noise on the client. The goal is to create visibility, find what matters, and produce a roadmap that actually helps.

01

Network Discovery

Identify routers, switches, workstations, wireless devices, cameras, printers, POS systems, and key endpoints.

02

Vulnerability Scan

Check for outdated software, exposed services, missing patches, and weak configurations.

03

Security Review

Review firewall posture, account security, remote access exposure, device status, and backup assumptions.

04

Remediation Roadmap

Deliver clear findings with prioritized next steps so the client knows what to fix first and why.

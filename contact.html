What Happens Next

Once your request is received, Lotus will review your information and schedule a short conversation to understand your environment and determine the best next step.

Most engagements follow the Lotus three-step security model:

  • Assess — evaluate systems, devices, and security posture
  • Harden — correct weaknesses and improve system configuration
  • Protect — establish stronger ongoing security practices

The goal is practical protection that improves real business environments without unnecessary complexity.

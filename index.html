How an assessment works

From discovery to action plan

A four-step process that gives you a clear picture of your security posture and a prioritised roadmap to improve it - delivered in 48 hours.

01

Network Discovery

Identify every router, switch, POS system, camera, and connected device across your environment.

02

Vulnerability Scan

Detect outdated software, missing patches, misconfigured services, and exposed attack surfaces.

03

Security Review

Analyse firewall rules, access permissions, remote access exposure, and compliance gaps.

04

Written Action Plan

Receive a prioritised, plain-language report outlining every finding, its risk level, and the recommended next steps to address it.

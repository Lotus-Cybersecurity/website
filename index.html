How an assessment works

From authorization to action plan

Every engagement follows the same disciplined process — starting with written authorization and ending with a plain-English action plan you can act on immediately. Nothing is scanned or tested without your explicit written approval.

01

Written Authorization

Before anything begins, we define the exact scope in writing. You authorize what gets tested, when, and how. Nothing happens outside that boundary.

02

Network Discovery

Identify every router, switch, POS system, workstation, and connected device across your authorized environment.

03

Vulnerability Scan

Detect outdated software, missing patches, misconfigured services, and exposed attack surfaces — within your defined scope.

04

Written Action Plan

A prioritised, plain-language report delivered within 48 hours. Every finding explained by risk level, business impact, and exactly what to do next.

How Lotus Works

1 — Security Assessment

We review your environment to understand what you have, where your risks are, and what needs attention first. This can include devices, user accounts, network exposure, basic security gaps, and overall security posture.

2 — Hardening and Cleanup

Once risks are identified, we help secure and organize your environment. That can include account protection, safer configurations, patching, device cleanup, access control improvements, and foundational safeguards that reduce exposure without overcomplicating your business.

3 — Monitoring and Support

After the initial work is done, Lotus can provide ongoing monitoring, visibility, reporting, and support so your business stays protected as it grows. The goal is simple: fewer blind spots, faster response, and more confidence.
